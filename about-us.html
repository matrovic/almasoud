About Almasoud

Almasoud Investments Limited is a growing trade and business enterprise specializing in the import and export of fresh produce and food products, alongside construction, tender bidding, and a range of activities carried out through our sister companies.

We began as a consultancy offering technical support to businesses in related sectors, before being incorporated in 2007 as a fully registered multi-business company. Today, we’re known for a competitive, quality-driven range of import/export services and products, operating under a group structure that includes four sister companies.
